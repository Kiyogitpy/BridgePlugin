# Kuradal/Duradel slayer task site candidates (research draft, in progress)

## Red Dragons (amount 30-65; req combat 68, partial Dragon Slayer I, "Seeing red" unlock (50 pts); required item: Anti-dragon shield / Dragonfire shield / Antifire potion(4), or Protect from Magic)
Targets: Red dragon (cb 152), Baby red dragon (cb 48) both count toward task. Brutal red dragon (cb 289, Catacombs of Kourend) is a separate, much slower alternative — not recommended.
Unattended difficulty: medium — dragonfire/melee hits hard without antifire uptime; manageable at a safespot with shield+potions or Protect from Magic.
1. Brimhaven Dungeon (Red dragon) — normal variant, cb 152; coords 2697,9506,0 (area, per SlayerLocations.java); spawns 16; single combat; safespot yes (next to pipe agility shortcut to black demons); access: Brimhaven tablet (2757,3179,0) then walk in — some routes need 30 Agility for a shortcut (unconfirmed if a no-agility route exists); needs: Anti-dragon shield/Dragonfire shield + Antifire potion(4), or Protect from Magic; hazards: dragonfire (melee+breath), adjacent black demons if using the agility shortcut.
2. Forthos Dungeon (Red dragon) — normal variant, cb 152; coords ~1812,9933,0; spawns 6; single combat; NOT cannonable; safespot yes (near fire/stalagmite by the strange-floor agility shortcut); access: walk from Kourend (no direct item teleport found — nearest is Rada's blessing: Kourend Woodland 1554,3461 or Xeric's talisman then walk); needs: same antidragon gear; hazards: dragonfire — but a nearby altar recharges Prayer indefinitely, making sustained Protect from Magic viable unattended (best safety option if the routine can prayer-flick).
3. Brimhaven Dungeon (Baby red dragon) — cb 48, weaker/safer; coords ~2696,9501,0; spawns 12; single combat; safespot no; access: same as #1; needs: same antidragon gear (lower risk since damage is much lower); hazards: dragonfire, but far less punishing than adult.
4. Catacombs of Kourend (Brutal red dragon) — cb 289; coords ~1611,10076,0; spawns 3; single combat; safespot no; access: Catacombs of Kourend entrance (Xeric's talisman family teleports nearby); needs: strong antidragon gear; hazards: long-range dragonfire + melee + magic, very high damage, NOT recommended for unattended play.
Recommend: 1 — Brimhaven Dungeon red dragon spot is the standard safespot with the largest population and a known access route; keep antifire uptime scripted.

## Skeletal Wyverns (amount 20-40; req Slayer 72, combat 70, completion of Elemental Workshop I; required item: Elemental shield / Mind shield / Dragonfire shield/ward / Ancient wyvern shield)
Targets: Skeletal Wyvern (cb 140) only — single NPC type, no higher-level variant.
Unattended difficulty: hard — aggressive, high max hits (13 melee/ranged, 50+ unmitigated icy breath which also disables retaliation while frozen); needs the required shield to cap breath damage and ideally Protect from Missiles.
1. Asgarnian Ice Dungeon, task-only upper area — cb 140; coords unknown exact (near main area ~3025,9543,0, "upper" section); spawns 14; single combat implied (not stated multi); safespot yes (dedicated safespot image referenced, exact tile unknown); access: no direct item teleport — Amulet of glory: Draynor Village (3105,3251,0) then walk south to Port Sarim and the trapdoor south of Port Sarim jail (~short walk), or fairy ring code AIQ (Mudskipper Point) after reaching any fairy ring first; needs: Elemental/Mind/Dragonfire shield or ward; hazards: icy breath freeze (blocks retaliation, still can eat/drink), all dungeon monsters aggressive, Steve/Pieve block entry unless actively on this task. Fewer competing players since only slayer-task players can enter — best pick for an unattended bot.
2. Asgarnian Ice Dungeon, main wyvern area — cb 140; coords 3025,9543,0 (LocLine cluster); spawns 8; single combat implied; safespot yes ("door safespot" — trap a wyvern behind the entrance after it loses aggression, which requires waiting ~10 minutes unaggro'd first, or attack from 7 tiles away under Protect from Missiles); access: same as #1; needs: same shield; hazards: same icy breath/aggro risk, plus other dungeon monsters (ice giants/warriors, pirates, hobgoblins) between entrance and the wyvern chamber, three agility shortcuts nearby (82/72/60 Agility, boostable, not required for this route) that a routine must not wander into.
Recommend: 1 — the task-only area is smaller (less contested) and has its own documented safespot; the 10-minute unaggro wait for #2's door safespot is a poor fit for unattended play, so treat #2 only as a fallback if #1's exact safespot tile can't be confirmed live.

## Smoke Devils (amount 130-200; req Slayer 93, combat 85; required item: Facemask or Slayer helmet)
Targets: Smoke devil (cb 160) counts; Nuclear smoke devil (superior, random spawn after "Bigger and Badder" unlock) also counts if it appears; Thermonuclear smoke devil is an optional boss-variant kill path, not required.
Unattended difficulty: hard — the ONLY location is a large multicombat room, not safespottable, and the wiki explicitly says a cannon is "strongly recommended" to stack devils (cannon is disallowed here); their magical-ranged attacks always deal at least moderate stat-draining damage without a facemask/slayer helm.
1. Smoke Devil Dungeon, main room — cb 160; coords 2351,9448,0 (LocLine start, cluster spans ~2351-2422,9421-9465); spawns 30 (36 total in dungeon incl. 6 near the boss room); multicombat; safespot no; access: task-only area (must have this task active to enter/be attacked) — Ring of dueling: Castle Wars (2440,3090,0) then walk south to the dungeon trapdoor, or fairy ring code bkp after reaching any fairy ring first; needs: Facemask/Slayer helmet, ranged or magic (magic/barrage recommended — high magic defence but low magic level, still fairly accurate; melee viable but slower per wiki); hazards: multicombat aggro from several devils at once with no safespot, no cannon to control the pull.
Recommend: 1 (only real option — the task has a single task-only location, so there is no site choice, only a tactic choice). Given the "no cannon" constraint, run this at reduced risk: fight near the room's edge/doorway to limit how many devils can reach the player at once rather than the wiki's cannon-stacking method, and keep HP margin high (magical ranged hits are frequent).

## Spiritual Creatures (amount 130-200, or 181-250 after "Spiritual fervour" unlock; req Slayer 63/68/83 (variant-gated), combat 80, Death Plateau quest, Strength or Agility 60; required item: none official, but a Saradomin- and Zamorak-aligned item are functionally required for tolerance)
Targets: Spiritual ranger (cb 115-127, Slayer 63+), Spiritual warrior (cb 115-134, needs Slayer 68+), Spiritual mage (cb 120-123, needs Slayer 83+, only NPC to drop dragon boots) — all three count; Bandos/Armadyl variants need extra Strength/Ranged 70 + hammer/crossbow, generally not worth it per wiki (rangers/warriors recommended to skip/block by many players due to low XP/poor drops).
Unattended difficulty: hard — every location is multicombat, and entering God Wars Dungeon drains stats/run energy unless "Fire of Unseasonal Warmth" (My Arm's Big Adventure) is built; wrong god-item loadout causes extra aggro from unrelated GWD followers.
1. God Wars Dungeon, main dungeon (Saradomin-area rangers) — cb ~122; coords 2891,5309,2 (ranger cluster; warrior/mage clusters nearby ~2886-2924,5267-5311,2); spawns 30 (rangers)/44 (warriors)/40 (mages); multicombat; safespot yes (documented brick safespot for Saradomin rangers, screenshot-guided, exact tile not machine-readable from this page); access: Trollheim tablet (2890,3679,0) then walk into the GWD entrance at Trollheim; needs: bring a Saradomin item AND a Zamorak item (Zamorak followers patrol the whole main dungeon) for tolerance, GWD entrance access itself (quest-gated, not detailed on this page — verify separately), Fire of Unseasonal Warmth built to avoid entry stat-drain; hazards: multicombat aggro from other gods' followers if tolerance items missing, several-monster pile-up risk.
2. God Wars Dungeon, Ancient Prison (Zaros variant) — cb 158-182, notably stronger; coords ~2881,5208,2; spawns 9 (rangers/warriors)/5 (mages); multicombat; safespot yes (per table, not detailed); access: same as #1, deeper — requires passing Saradomin/Zamorak followers to reach the Ancient Prison; needs: The Frozen Door miniquest (70 Str/HP/Ranged/Agility) plus a Zaros item, recommend also Saradomin+Zamorak items for the approach; hazards: much harder hitting (arrow that disables prayer + binds, AoE smoke damage up to 40, poison/attack-drain/freeze secondary effects that bypass prayer) — not recommended for unattended.
3. Wilderness God Wars Dungeon — cb same ranges as #1; coords 3025,10124,0 / 3029,10127,0 (mage cluster); spawns 12 each type; multicombat; safespot yes (per table); access: no safe item teleport into the Wilderness dungeon area found — routine would need a wilderness-specific route; needs: one Armadyl-or-Bandos item (entrance-dependent) + Saradomin + Zamorak items; hazards: WILDERNESS (level 28-32), PK risk on top of the multicombat/god-aggro hazards — AVOID per the no-wilderness preference; only the regular GWD (#1/#2) is needed here since it is a real alternative.
Recommend: 1 — God Wars Dungeon main dungeon (Saradomin ranger safespot) is the only non-wilderness, documented-safespot option that doesn't require an extra miniquest; confirm the exact safespot tile live before running unattended.

## Suqahs (amount 60-90, or 162-250 extended; req combat 85, partial Lunar Diplomacy; required item: none)
Targets: Suqah (cb 111) — single NPC type, no variants/higher-level alternative.
Unattended difficulty: easy — no required item, account already needs Lunar Diplomacy for the Lunar spellbook, and a melee-only single-combat spot exists.
1. South-west Lunar Isle — cb 111; coords ~2106,3866,0 (cluster 2064-2106,3866-3886); spawns 6; single combat ({{No}} multicombat, "single-way combat" per map label); safespot yes; access: Lunar isle teleport (2094,3913,0, requires partial Lunar Diplomacy — already satisfied) then walk south; needs: none; hazards: melee-only here (per wiki prose "these attack with both melee and magic" applies to the north group, not this one) — lowest-risk of the three sites.
2. North Lunar Isle — cb 111; coords 2096,3942,0 (cluster 2096-2160,3926-3951); spawns 16 (largest population); multicombat; safespot yes (stand next to the hill, attack with ranged); access: same Lunar isle teleport, lands right at this cluster; needs: none, but Protect from Magic recommended (melee + magic attacks); hazards: multicombat, magic damage without prayer.
3. South-east Lunar Isle — cb 111; coords ~2132,3871,0 (cluster 2120-2141,3853-3871); spawns 4; multicombat; safespot yes; access: same teleport, walk south; needs: none; hazards: multicombat, smallest population, no clear throughput advantage over #1/#2.
Recommend: 1 — South-west Lunar Isle is single combat, safespottable, melee-only (no prayer-swap logic needed), and reached by the same Lunar Isle teleport already assumed available for the account's spellbook.

## Trolls (amount 130-200; req combat 60; required item: none for mountain trolls melee route; Neitiznot shield recommended for ice trolls)
Targets: Mountain troll (cb 69-124 variants incl. Thrower troll/Troll general/Rock/Stick/Pee Hat/Kraka), Ice troll (grunt/male/female/runt, cb 74-124, Fremennik Isles/Ice Path) — both families count; bosses Dad/Arrg/Ice Troll King also count but are one-offs, not farmable.
Unattended difficulty: easy-medium — mountain trolls hit only melee (fully negated by Protect from Melee), no quest needed for the standard melee route; ice trolls need a Neitiznot shield and hit ranged too.
1. Death Plateau — Mountain troll, cb 69/71/91/104/111 variants; coords 2852,3591,0 (cluster 2852-2876,3584-3595); spawns 15 (largest population); single combat; safespot yes (troll-built rock structures block LOS); access: Games necklace: Burthorpe (2899,3553,0) then walk north through the canyon to the plateau; needs: none (no quest requirement for the melee route); hazards: thrower trolls in the approach canyon throw rocks — Protect from Missiles recommended while walking in, not needed once safespotted.
2. Troll Stronghold, outside — Mountain troll, cb ~69-113; coords 2843,3675,0 (cluster 2843-2856,3673-3684); spawns 8; single combat; safespot yes; access: Trollheim tablet (2890,3679,0), short walk to the Stronghold exterior; needs: none; hazards: melee only, straightforward — good throughput-vs-safety fallback if Death Plateau is contested.
3. Keldagrim entrance tunnel — Mountain troll, cb 69 only (melee-only subset); coords 2825,10101,0 (cluster 2825-2844,10083-10101); spawns 7; single combat; safespot yes (rocks at passage sides); access: no direct item teleport found — nearest is a Keldagrim-area teleport/minecart, else walk from Ferox/Trollheim; needs: none; hazards: narrow passage (irrelevant with no cannon), melee-only so simplest damage profile of all candidates.
4. Northern Jatizso/Neitiznot isles — Ice troll female/male/grunt/runt, cb 74-102; coords 2367,3868,0; spawns 15 female/14 male/4 grunt/19 runt (largest total pop); multicombat; safespot yes; access: Fremennik sea boots teleport (2643,3675,0) or Enchanted lyre: Jatizso/Neitiznot (2409,3809,0 / 2336,3802,0) then boat/walk to the northern isle; needs: completion of The Fremennik Trials (for Jatizso access), Neitiznot shield strongly recommended (ice troll females hit hard without it); hazards: multicombat, ranged damage from females, stat-draining trolls nearby in the Ice Path (avoid straying there).
Recommend: 1 — Death Plateau has the largest single-combat population, a documented safespot, no quest gate, and a simple Games necklace + short walk route.

## TzHaar (amount 130-199; req none beyond unlocking "Hot stuff" for 100 Slayer reward points; required item: none)
Targets: TzHaar-Hur (cb74), TzHaar-Mej (cb100, magic), TzHaar-Xil (cb120, melee or melee/ranged depending on weapon), TzHaar-Ket (cb149 outer / cb221 inner city, needs fire cape shown once to enter inner) — all four count. The slayer master may ALSO offer TzTok-Jad or TzKal-Zuk in place of a normal kill — must be declined/ignored for an unattended bot.
Unattended difficulty: hard — city aggro rules are a real trap: attacking a Hur pulls every nearby TzHaar, attacking a Mej pulls nearby Xil+Ket, so target SELECTION matters as much as location; no location-comparison table/coords exist beyond the general city area.
1. Mor Ul Rek, outer city (melee TzHaar-Ket or melee-only TzHaar-Xil) — cb 149 (Ket) / 120 (Xil); coords ~2439,5133,0 (per SlayerLocations.java; page gives no finer coords); spawns unknown ("?"); multicombat area generally, but wiki says Ket/melee-Xil "are easily safespotted through the many vents" — effectively single-target if positioned correctly; safespot yes (vent terrain, exact tiles unconfirmed); access: Amulet of glory: Karamja (2918,3176,0) then walk to the Karamja Volcano entrance and down into Mor Ul Rek, or (if owned) Achievement diary cape teleport "9. TzHaar-Mej" (2455,5133,0) lands directly in the city; needs: none required, Ring of wealth optional for gem drops; hazards: picking a Hur or Mej by mistake chain-aggros the whole area — a routine MUST filter target NPC by exact name/id (Ket or melee-Xil only), and must ignore/decline any TzTok-Jad or TzKal-Zuk task-swap offer (fighting either unattended is not viable).
2. Mor Ul Rek, inner city (TzHaar-Ket, cb221) — stronger variant with obsidian armour drop chance; coords same city area, inner section; spawns unknown; same multicombat/vent-safespot situation; access: same as #1, plus a one-time fire cape show to the gate guard; needs: fire cape (quest-locked behind Fight Caves completion) — not assumed available; hazards: same aggro rules, higher damage output than the outer Ket.
Recommend: 1 — outer-city melee Ket/Xil is the standard safe pick and needs no fire cape; the routine must hard-code target-name filtering (avoid Hur/Mej) and refuse the Jad/Zuk task-swap prompt, since this is the one task on this list where a wrong click can trigger a boss fight, not just extra trash damage.

## Vampyres (amount 100-210, or 200-250 after "More at stake" unlock; req combat 35, Priest in Peril completion, "Actual Vampyre Slayer" unlock (80 pts) for Kuradal/Duradel to assign it; required item: none for tier-1 (Feral Vampyre); Silverlight/silver weapon or Efaritay's aid for tier-2 (Juvinate); Ivandis/Blisterwood/Hallowed flail or Sunspear for tier-3 (Vyrewatch/Sentinel/Venator))
Targets: Feral Vampyre (cb 61-130, any weapon, tier 1) counts everywhere; Vampyre Juvinate (cb 50-119, needs silver/Efaritay's aid, tier 2); Vyrewatch/Vyrewatch Sentinel/Venator (cb 87-246, needs a flail-class weapon, tier 3, higher Slayer/quest-gated). A routine without tier-2/3 gear should simply target Feral Vampyre, which is legal anywhere they spawn.
Unattended difficulty: easy (Feral Vampyre route) to medium (Sentinel route) — Feral Vampyres take any weapon and are straightforward; Sentinels hit harder but only via melee and are described by the wiki as easy to leave running with minimal input.
1. Haunted Woods (Feral Vampyre) — cb 61; coords 3614,3431,0 (cluster spans 3585-3646,3431-3514); spawns 19 (largest Feral cluster); single combat; safespot yes (documented tile 3592,3508,0 near fairy ring ALQ); access: no direct item teleport found — reach any fairy ring first then code ALQ (lands mid-Haunted Woods), or walk from Canifis; needs: Priest in Peril (already required for the task); hazards: always-aggressive (fine for unattended engagement), nearby Leeches drain stats and do NOT show on the minimap — keep the routine's roam radius tight to the safespot.
2. Burgh de Rott, west (Feral Vampyre + Vampyre Juvinate) — cb 61 (Feral) / 54 (Juvinate); coords 3458,3225,0 (cluster 3458-3462,3225-3240); spawns 6 Feral + 15 Juvinate (21 total, largest combined); single combat; safespot yes; access: Morytania legs tablet: Burgh de Rott (3482,3230,0) — a direct teleport into this exact area; needs: In Aid of the Myreque completion; hazards: none notable beyond needing silver/Efaritay's aid to legally damage the Juvinates (target Feral only if that gear is unavailable).
3. Darkmeyer, west of the Arboretum (Vyrewatch Sentinel) — cb 151, highest value (blood shard drop); coords ~3651,3379,0 (cluster 3592-3663,3324-3393); spawns 19 (most Sentinels of any site, only site with them); single combat; NOT cannonable, NOT safespottable — but the wiki notes Sentinels are permanently aggressive and melee-only, "easily AFK'ed with little input"; access: Drakan's medallion: Darkmeyer (3592,3337,0) teleport lands right in the area, near a bank/altar; needs: Sins of the Father started (Slayer 50 to begin), an Ivandis/Blisterwood/Hallowed flail or Sunspear, Vyre noble clothing if wanting them NON-aggressive instead (not needed here since aggression is the point); hazards: melee-only but no safespot, so straight tank-and-spank — verify HP margin/food logic is solid before running unattended, and note bank tellers refuse service without a disguise until The Blood Moon Rises' 3rd Drakan fight is done (Hallowed Sepulchre lobby bank chest as a workaround).
4. Meiyerditch (Vyrewatch) — cb 87-125; coords 3624,3322,0 (cluster 3593-3638,3182-3322); spawns 61 Vyrewatch (largest population by far); single combat; NOT cannonable/safespottable; access: Drakan's medallion covers this region too (nearest teleport, then walk), or Meiyerditch is adjacent to Darkmeyer; needs: Darkness of Hallowvale started, plus the same tier-3 flail weapon (weapon itself unlocked via A Taste of Hope, Slayer 38 to begin); hazards: same tank-and-spank profile as Darkmeyer, no teleport as convenient as Drakan's medallion for Darkmeyer.
Recommend: 1 — Haunted Woods needs no special weapon and has a documented safespot, making it the lowest-friction default; switch to 3 (Darkmeyer Sentinels) if the account already owns a flail-class weapon and has started Sins of the Father, since the wiki explicitly calls that spot AFK-friendly and it is far more valuable per kill.

## Warped Creatures (amount 130-200; req Slayer 56, "Warped Reality" unlock (60 pts), completion of The Path of Glouphrie; required item: Crystal chime (mandatory for ANY warped creature — without it they are invulnerable); Earmuffs or Slayer helmet additionally needed only for Warped Terrorbirds)
Targets: Warped Tortoise (cb 121, melee-only, NOT aggressive) and Warped Terrorbird (cb 96, melee+ranged, aggressive, switches style vs your prayer, never loses aggression) both count; Mutated Tortoise/Terrorbird are superior random-spawn variants after "Bigger and Badder", not required.
Unattended difficulty: easy (tortoise-only route) — the wiki's own "Prayer Melee (Tortoise)" gear setup is explicitly built to negate all damage via Protect from Melee and "increase AFK time"; terrorbirds are considerably riskier and best avoided for an unattended bot.
1. Poison Waste Dungeon, main level, entrance tortoises — Warped Tortoise, cb 121; coords ~1487,4291,1 (entrance cluster, part of the 1479-1519,4237-4282 group); spawns 14 on this level; multicombat area but tortoises are non-aggressive melee-only (Protect from Melee negates all damage — the wiki's dedicated AFK setup); safespot yes (also safespottable further in, behind pillars near 1505,4282,1); access: Prifddinas tablet (3239,6077,0) then spirit-tree/walk to the Poison Waste Dungeon entrance (exact route not detailed on this page); needs: Crystal chime; hazards: must not wander toward the Warped Terrorbird spawns (aggressive, permanent aggro) — stay at/near the entrance tortoise cluster.
2. Poison Waste Dungeon, lower level tortoises — Warped Tortoise, cb 121; coords ~1352,4235,0 / 1526-1527,4241-4249,0; spawns 3 (much smaller); multicombat; safespot yes; access: same as #1, one level down; needs: Crystal chime; hazards: same as #1 but far fewer targets — only useful as overflow.
3. Poison Waste Dungeon, main level (Warped Terrorbird, ranged/magic safespot near entrance doors) — cb 96; coords ~1487,4291,1 (same cluster as tortoises); spawns 33 (largest population in the dungeon); multicombat; cannonable; safespot "maybe" (documented doorway safespot needs a 9+ tile range weapon, e.g. Crystal bow/Craw's bow, or magic); access: same as #1; needs: Crystal chime + Earmuffs/Slayer helmet (halves max hit, blunts ranged chip damage); hazards: aggressive, never loses aggression, switches attack style to counter whatever protection prayer is active — much higher management overhead, not recommended unattended unless the exact safespot tile is confirmed live and a long-range weapon is available.
Recommend: 1 — entrance-level Warped Tortoises with Protect from Melee is the wiki's own purpose-built AFK method; it needs only the Crystal chime and avoids the terrorbirds entirely.

## Waterfiends (amount 130-200; req combat 75, granted access to the Ancient Cavern; required item: none officially, but Protect from Missiles / high Magic Defence gear strongly recommended since they hit with Magic + magical Ranged, not blockable by melee prayers)
Targets: Waterfiend (cb 115) — single NPC type, no higher-level alternative; wiki itself calls them a task worth considering skipping (moderately high stats, mediocre drops) but that's a value judgement, not a safety one.
Unattended difficulty: medium — no protection prayer fully negates their damage (both attacks roll vs Magic Defence), so sustained unattended fighting needs solid Magic Defence gear or a genuine safespot.
1. Ancient Cavern — cb 115; coords 1738,5343,0 (cluster 1738-1767,5340-5363); spawns 16; single combat; safespot yes (4-tile attack range — stand across the river and hit with Ranged/Magic, or wait out the 10-minute aggression timer at a documented pillar spot); access: Games necklace: Barbarian Outpost (2520,3571,0) then a short walk/dive through the whirlpool into the Ancient Cavern; needs: none; hazards: none beyond the standard magic/ranged damage — best candidate given the safespot and simple teleport.
2. Iorwerth Dungeon — cb 115; coords 3161,12457,0 (cluster spans 3161-3192,12445-12476); spawns 20 (largest population); single combat; cannonable; safespot NO; access: Teleport crystal: Lletya (2330,3171,0) or Prifddinas (3264,6066,0, needs Song of the Elves) then walk in; needs: Song of the Elves completion; hazards: no safespot means continuous unmitigated magic/ranged damage — riskier unattended than #1 despite the larger population.
3. Kraken Cove — cb 115; coords 2248,9994,0 (cluster 2248-2287,9987-9997); spawns 15; single combat; NOT cannonable, NOT safespottable; access: fairy ring code AKQ after reaching any fairy ring first (task-only area); needs: none beyond the task itself; hazards: same no-safespot risk as #2, with a less convenient teleport chain (fairy ring hop required).
Recommend: 1 — Ancient Cavern is the only site with a genuine safespot against Waterfiends' unblockable magic/ranged damage, and the Games necklace teleport is the simplest access of the three.

## Wyrms (amount 100-160, or 200-250 extended; req Slayer 62; required item: Boots of stone / Boots of brimstone / Granite boots, unless Elite Kourend & Kebos Diary completed)
Targets: Wyrm (cb 97-99, melee+magic) counts; Wyrmling (cb 55, Neypotzli, melee-only, lower value, only drops wyrmling bones) is an easier alternative; Shadow wyrm (superior, random post-"Bigger and Badder") also counts if it spawns; Lava Strykewyrm (cb 116, Charred Dungeon) is a valid task alternative but needs 60 Sailing.
Unattended difficulty: easy — attacking a standard wyrm from outside melee range forces it to use ONLY magic, and Protect from Magic then negates all of its damage; a required heat-floor boot item is cheap and simple to enforce.
1. Karuulm Slayer Dungeon, task-only area (south batch) — Wyrm, cb 97-99; coords ~1252-1273,10147-10159,0 (7 of the 16 total, task-restricted so fewer competing players); single combat; NOT cannonable; safespot yes (documented: lure the wyrm next to Garbek quo Maten from the marked white tile, then attack from the marked blue tile); access: Rada's blessing: Mount Karuulm (1311,3806,0) teleports directly to the dungeon entrance, then walk down to the lower level; needs: Boots of stone/brimstone/Granite boots (or Elite Kourend Diary); hazards: none beyond the heat-floor requirement — best pick, restricted population plus a documented safespot.
2. Karuulm Slayer Dungeon, main batch — Wyrm, cb 97-99; coords 1260,10192,0 (cluster 1260-1280,10179-10197); single combat; NOT cannonable; safespot yes (same general strategy); spawns 9; access: same as #1; needs: same boots; hazards: shared with any other player also on a wyrm task (not task-restricted like the southern batch) — use only if #1 is contested.
3. Neypotzli, Earthbound Cavern — Wyrmling, cb 55 (half the HP/Defence of a normal wyrm, melee-only); coords ~1366,9708,0 (cluster 1364-1370,9694-9708); spawns 6; single combat; cannonable; safespot yes (documented, between the statue and the stairs); access: teleport route not found in the vendored TSV (Neypotzli is reached via Moons of Peril content) — unknown, verify live; needs: none noted, free food/prayer-restore/potions available on site; hazards: none, but only drops wyrmling bones (low value) — fine purely for task completion, weaker fallback if Karuulm is unreachable.
4. Charred Dungeon — Lava Strykewyrm, cb 116 (ranged+melee, not magic); coords ~2733,8886,0; spawns 12; single combat; NOT cannonable; safespot yes (needs a 9-tile-range weapon); access: requires a boat from Void Knights' Outpost or Red Rock; needs: 60 Sailing (Sailing-gated per the "unsuitable" list) — SKIP unless the account has 60 Sailing.
Recommend: 1 — Karuulm's task-only southern wyrm batch combines a documented safespot, a direct item teleport (Rada's blessing: Mount Karuulm), and a restricted population, with damage fully negatable via Protect from Magic at range.
