# Aya/Turael slayer task kill sites

Account is Lunar spellbook (NPC Contact only) — no standard-spellbook teleports assumed.
Only item teleports (tablets/jewellery) and existing repo coordinates are used. Wilderness
sites are excluded from every "Recommend" pick unless explicitly noted as a last resort.

## Banshees (amount 15-30; req: Slayer 15, Combat 20, Priest in Peril; required item: earmuffs or Slayer helmet)
Targets: Banshee (23), Screaming banshee (70, superior), Twisted Banshee (89), Screaming twisted banshee (144, superior)
1. Slayer Tower, ground floor — Banshee, level 23; coords 3429,3564,0; spawns 8; single; access: Slayer ring: Tower teleport (3421,3537,0), very short walk north-east; needs: earmuffs/Slayer helmet (task-required), Priest in Peril (task-required, also gates this teleport option); hazards: none
2. Catacombs of Kourend — Twisted Banshee, level 89; coords 1608,9994,0; spawns 6; multi; access: no item teleport lands adjacent to the Catacombs entrance in the sourced list — walk in from Kourend Castle; needs: earmuffs/Slayer helmet; hazards: none noted, shares the room with Ankou
Recommend: 1 — closest teleport-to-spawn distance, single combat, already gated by the same Priest in Peril requirement the task itself needs.

## Bats (amount 15-30; req: Combat 5; required item: none)
Targets: Bat (6), Giant bat (27), Albino bat (52, quest + light source)
1. Silvarea, north of Digsite — Bat, level 6; coords 3341,3478,0; spawns 13; single; access: Digsite pendant: Digsite teleport (3341,3445,0), short walk north; needs: none; hazards: none
2. Slayer Tower entrance — Bat, level 6; coords 3418,3522,0; spawns 6; single; access: Slayer ring: Tower teleport (3421,3537,0), very short walk north; needs: none; hazards: none
3. Taverley Dungeon — Giant bat, level 27; coords 2904,9832,0; spawns 9; single; access: Taverley teleport tablet (2893,3465,0), short walk to dungeon entrance then descend; needs: none; hazards: none
Recommend: 1 — this is the assignment table's own "Location (Slayer Point Farming)" pick, has the most spawns of the three and sits right on top of the Digsite pendant landing.

1. Fremennik Province, woods outside Rellekka — Bear cub (15) / Grizzly bear (21); coords 2689,3612,0; spawns 10; single; access: Rellekka teleport tablet (2670,3631,0), short walk east/north-east; needs: none; hazards: none
2. Outside the Mind Altar — Black bear, level 19; coords 2967,3480,0; spawns 4; single; access: Mind Altar teleport tablet (2979,3510,0), short walk south-west; needs: none; hazards: none
3. Great Kourend, Farming Guild — Bear cub/Grizzly/Black bear mix; coords 1219,3679,0; spawns 4; single; access: Skills necklace: Farming Guild (1248,3719,0), short walk; needs: none; hazards: none
Recommend: 1 — highest spawn count (10) of the three, and the Rellekka tablet lands within easy running distance.

## Cave bugs (amount 10-30; req: Slayer 7; required item: light source, e.g. bullseye lantern (lit))
Targets: Cave bug (6), Cave bug (96, Dorgesh-Kaan variant, much stronger)
1. Lumbridge Swamp Caves — Cave bug, level 6; coords 3149,9578,0; spawns unknown (cluster of ~20 pins across the cave); single; access: Ring of the elements: Water Altar teleport (3170,3155,0), short walk to the swamp cave entrance then descend; needs: light source (required), a rope on first entry to the hole; hazards: wall beasts nearby will grab and attack if in their line of sight
2. Dorgesh-Kaan South Dungeon (fairy ring code AJQ) — Cave bug, level 96; coords 2712,5233,0; spawns unknown (~14 pins); single; access: no item teleport in the sourced list reaches Dorgesh-Kaan directly; the assignment table's own farming pick, but only reachable via fairy ring AJQ or Death to the Dorgeshuun's cave-train route; needs: light source, Death to the Dorgeshuun completed; hazards: much higher combat level than the swamp-cave variant
Recommend: 1 — simpler access (a single item teleport plus a short walk), lower combat level, no quest gate beyond the task's own Slayer 7.

## Kalphite (amount 15-30; req: Combat 15; required item: none)
Targets: Kalphite worker (28), Kalphite soldier (85, poison from 4hp), Kalphite guardian (141, poison from 6hp)
1. Kalphite Cave (task-exclusive area) — Kalphite worker/soldier/guardian mix; coords 3337,9514,0; spawns 43 (18 worker + 20 soldier + 5 guardian); multi; access: Desert amulet 4: Kalphite cave teleport (3322,3122,0), very short walk to the entrance then descend; needs: none, no rope required; hazards: poison from soldiers/guardians (antipoison or Protect from Melee recommended)
2. Kalphite Lair — all variants incl. Kalphite Queen; coords 3220,9525,0; spawns 25 (13 worker + 7 soldier + 4 guardian + 1 Queen); multi; access: no adjacent item teleport in the sourced list — long walk from Al Kharid; needs: a rope unless the Hard Desert Diary is complete; hazards: poison

Note: SlayerLocations.java's only entry for this category points at the Kalphite LAIR (3220,9525, matching candidate 2 above), not the Cave — the wiki's own comparison table favours the Cave for a farming task.

## Lizards (amount 15-30; req: Slayer 22; required item: ice cooler, for Small/Desert/regular Lizard)
Targets: Small lizard (12), Desert lizard (24), Lizard (42) — all need an ice cooler; Sulphur lizard (50, Slayer 44, needs heat-protection boots instead)
1. Kharidian Desert, north of Nardah (fairy ring code DLQ) — Small/Desert lizard mix; coords 3405,3059,0; spawns 43 (31 small + 7 desert + 5 regular); single; access: Nardah teleport tablet (3419,2917,0) or Desert amulet 4: Nardah (3426,2927,0), walk north (~100 tiles); needs: ice cooler (required to finish kills), waterskins recommended, desert heat protection; hazards: desert heat damage
2. Karuulm Slayer Dungeon (1st floor) — Sulphur lizard, level 50; coords 1194,10142,0; spawns 13; single; access: no item teleport in the sourced list is close; needs: Slayer 44 (above this task's own requirement), boots of stone/brimstone/granite for heat protection; hazards: extreme heat damage without the right boots
Recommend: 1 — this is the assignment table's own farming pick and the only one reachable with a plain item teleport; the Sulphur lizard alternative needs a higher Slayer level than the task itself grants access to.

## Skeletons (amount 15-30; req: Combat 15; required item: none)
Targets: Skeleton (21-85, common across many dungeons)
1. Catacombs of Kourend — Skeleton; coords 1636,10046,0; spawns 6; multi; access: Xeric's talisman: Xeric's Heart teleport (1644,3673,0), the Catacombs entrance is close by at Kourend Castle; needs: none; hazards: none; bonus: bones can be buried here for Prayer, and kills here also grant Ancient shards
2. Edgeville Dungeon — Skeleton; coords unknown; spawns 19 (+7 more in an adjoining wilderness pocket, avoid that part); single; access: Amulet of glory: Edgeville (3087,3496,0), short walk then descend; needs: none; hazards: the wiki explicitly warns the northern part of this dungeon is a PK risk zone — stay in the main section only
3. Digsite Dungeon — Skeleton; coords unknown; spawns 10; single; access: Digsite pendant: Digsite teleport (3341,3445,0), short walk then descend; needs: partial completion of The Digsite quest; hazards: none
Recommend: 1 — multicombat, no PK risk (Catacombs of Kourend is not in the wilderness, unlike Edgeville Dungeon's edge), and Xeric's talisman lands close to the entrance.

## Zombies (amount 15-30; req: Combat 10; required item: none)
Targets: Undead chicken (1, Priest in Peril), Undead cow (2, Priest in Peril), Zombie (13-76, common)
1. Alice's farm, west of the Ectofuntus — Undead cow (2) / Undead chicken (1); coords unknown (wiki gives no maplink for this entry); spawns 10; single; access: Ectophial teleport (3660,3522,0) lands at Port Phasmatys, right next to the Ectofuntus; needs: Priest in Peril completed; hazards: none, very low combat levels
2. Stronghold of Security, Catacomb of Famine — Zombie, levels 13-76; coords unknown; spawns 52 (largest, split across three groups); single; access: Falador teleport tablet (2964,3378,0), enter the Stronghold and descend two floors; needs: navigate multiple floors down; hazards: the level-76 zombies here are much stronger than the low end of the range
3. Varrock Sewers — Zombie; coords unknown; spawns 6; multi; access: Varrock teleport tablet (3213,3424,0), short walk then descend; needs: none; hazards: none
Recommend: 1 — very low combat levels, minimal travel via the Ectophial, and matches both the assignment table's and SlayerLocations.java's underlying area (Morytania/Ectofuntus). Important disagreement: SlayerLocations.java's only coded entry for this category is "Graveyard of Shadows, north of Ferox Enclave" (3152,3674, wilderness level 18-21) — that is a WILDERNESS site and must not be used given this document's instruction to avoid the wilderness; Alice's Farm (non-wilderness) was used instead.
