# Kuradal/Duradel slayer task site candidates

## Boss (amount 3-35, up to 65 w/ Combat Achievements; req: unlock "Like a boss" 200 pts + per-boss reqs; required item: varies per boss)
Recommend: SKIP — two-roll boss task behind the 200-point "Like a boss" unlock; out of scope for an unattended routine.

## Cave Horrors (amount 130-200, 200-250 with "Horrorific" 100-pt unlock; req Slayer 58, Combat 85, completion of Cabin Fever; required item: Witchwood icon (melee range only) + a light source (Bullseye lantern lit), unless Fire of Eternal Light already built)
Targets: Cave horror (combat 80, 55 xp) — the only counting variant; Cave abomination (combat 206, superior, spawns after "Bigger and Badder" perk, not separately targetable)
Unattended difficulty: medium — single-combat, safespottable/cannonable, but needs a light source and (for melee) a Witchwood icon vs. a magic-melee special attack that always hits 10% of max HP
1. Mos Le'Harmless Cave, gallery safespot near entrance — Cave horror lvl 80; coords 3777,9449,0; spawns 70 (cluster of ~70 spawn tiles per wiki LocLine data); single; safespot yes (several gallery spots: behind Fire of Eternal Light at entrance, enclosed area past the fire, lakeside spots farther in); access: item teleport "Mos le'harmless teleport" scroll → lands 3701,2996 (surface, north gate), short walk to cave entrance 3748,2973, enter, then to spawn cluster; needs: Cabin Fever completed (also the task's own unlock req), light source, Book o' piracy not required for the cave itself; hazards: magic-based melee special attack (10% max HP) if fought at melee range without Witchwood icon/Protect from Melee — avoided entirely from a safespot
2. Mos Le'Harmless Cave, cannon safespot (cited on-wiki) — Cave horror lvl 80; coords 3785,9460,0 (cannon placement) with player standing one tile off per the wiki's described tile; spawns 70 (same cluster); single; safespot yes (monster pathing gets stuck on the wall, confirmed on-wiki); access: same teleport scroll + walk as above, deeper into the cave; needs: same as above, ranged weapon + optional dwarf cannon (routine has no cannon per constraints, so plain ranged safespot only); hazards: same magic-melee special, avoided from the tile
Recommend: 1 — safespottable, single-combat, cannon-free ranged safespot right by the entrance minimizes travel and walking-into-melee-range risk; only needs a light source plus the standard Cabin Fever unlock the task already requires

## Cave Kraken (amount 100-120, 150-200 with "Krack on" 100-pt unlock; req Slayer 87, Magic 50, Combat 80; required item: none)
Targets: Cave kraken (combat 127, 125 xp); Kraken (combat 291, boss variant, 255 xp) — Kraken chamber requires fishing explosives to initiate
Unattended difficulty: hard — the monster is fought almost purely with Magic (earth spells, elemental weakness); it takes reduced Ranged damage and the Noxious halberd (melee reach) deals NO damage at all, so this is effectively magic-only content, and the account is on the LUNAR spellbook with no standard-spellbook attack spells — earth spells are not castable without a spellbook switch per trip
1. Kraken Cove, cave kraken area — Cave kraken lvl 127; coords 2272,10016,0 (mapID 19, per wiki map link; no fixed spawn tile — surfaces from whirlpools scattered around the cove, SlayerLocations.java confirms "no fixed spawn tile exists"); spawns 25 (whirlpool-triggered, not static); multi: not stated by source; safespot ?: not stated; access: item teleport "Piscatoris teleport" scroll → lands 2339,3649, run SW ~70 tiles to Cave Entrance 2277,3611,0, enter; alternative fairy ring code AKQ lands just east of the entrance; needs: none extra (task itself requires Magic 50/Slayer 87/Combat 80 already); hazards: effectively Magic-only monster (see difficulty note), Lieve McCracken blocks off-task attacks so must be on this exact task
2. Kraken Cove, Kraken boss chamber — Kraken lvl 291 (boss variant, counts for the task); coords 2272,10016,0 (same cove, boss room further in); spawns 1 (single boss encounter, re-enter to re-fight); single; safespot ?: page notes Kraken's attacks are "typeless" so "cannot be prayed against" — a hazard for an unattended routine with no active tank-and-spank logic; access: same as above, walk to boss room, bring fishing explosives to initiate; needs: fishing explosives (obtainable from any Slayer Master per wiki); hazards: typeless unpray-able damage, Magic-only effective damage same as above
Recommend: SKIP — both variants are effectively Magic-only kills (earth spells vs. reduced ranged/zero melee), which conflicts with the account's Lunar-only spellbook; would need a per-trip spellbook switch that this routine architecture doesn't support, and the boss variant's unpray-able typeless damage adds unattended risk on top

## Dagannoth (amount 130-200; req Combat 75, completion of Horror from the Deep; required item: none)
Targets: Dagannoth (combat 74/92, weaker than level suggests; level-74s at Catacombs/Prison/Lighthouse all attack melee-only, some level-74s elsewhere attack range); Dagannoth (Waterbirth Island variant, combat 88/90); Dagannoth spawn (42, Waterbirth only, not recommended — lower xp); Dagannoth Kings (303, deep Waterbirth, needs extra prep) — none need higher Slayer level, just Combat 75 overall task req
Unattended difficulty: easy — Catacombs/Prison variants are melee-only and much weaker than their combat level, with known safespots
1. Catacombs of Kourend, southern section — Dagannoth lvl 74/92; coords 1664,10000,0 (mapID 32); spawns 14; multi (dwarf cannon banned dungeon-wide, so no cannon competition); safespot yes (3 documented methods: aggro-box edge-standing on lvl 74 tiles then corner-safespotting lvl 92s once aggro expires; luring around a corner; using a non-aggressive Warped Jelly as a blocker); access: item teleport "Xeric's talisman: Xeric's Heart" → 1644,3673 (outside the King Rada I statue, the main Catacombs entrance at 1639,3674), enter, walk to southern section; needs: none beyond the task's own Horror from the Deep; hazards: multicombat area (several dagannoth can reach you at once away from the safespot), bring bones/seed box (dagannoth drop herb seeds; burying bones restores Prayer here)
2. Jormungand's Prison — Dagannoth lvl 74/92 (melee-only here); coords 2433,10417,0 (mapID 51); spawns 62 (largest single-location count); multi, cannonable yes (not usable per this routine's no-cannon constraint), safespot yes; access: no direct item teleport found in vendor TSV — nearest is a Fremennik-area teleport (e.g. Fremennik sea boots → 2643,3675) then walk/boat to the prison entrance (walk distance not measured); needs: The Fremennik Exiles completed (hard gate — only accessible after that quest); hazards: melee-only so predictable, but multicombat with 62 spawns is a dense pack
3. Lighthouse Dungeon — Dagannoth lvl 74/92; coords 2511,10020,0 (mapID 10097, matches existing SlayerLocations.java entry); spawns 22; multi, cannonable yes, safespot NO (page explicitly marks not safespottable); access: walk from the Lighthouse (Fremennik Isles) surface entrance, no dedicated item teleport found; needs: none beyond task req; hazards: not safespottable so melee/prayer-tank exposure is direct, and the page notes the area is "usually busy with players using a dwarf cannon" — crowding risk for an unattended routine competing for spawns
4. Waterbirth Island Dungeon — Dagannoth lvl 88/90 (stronger variant) + Dagannoth spawn (42) + Dagannoth Kings (303, deep); coords: unknown (no LocLine given, "Maplink" cell empty on the wiki table); spawns 93 Dagannoth + 3 Kings; multi, cannonable yes, safespot "maybe, see notes"; access: entrance area is NOT multicombat — reaching the real multicombat zone needs a pet rock or Humphrey Dumphrey plus a ranged weapon or another player's help; needs: extra dungeon-specific prep just to reach the main area; hazards: strongest dagannoth variant, entrance mechanic actively blocks solo/simple access — poor fit for unattended
Recommend: 1 — Catacombs of Kourend: melee-only dagannoth, three documented safespot methods, a clean item-teleport landing right outside the entrance, no quest gate beyond the task's own requirement, and cannon is banned dungeon-wide so there's no player-cannon crowding to compete with

## Dark Beasts (amount 10-20, 110-135 with "Need More Darkness" 100-pt unlock; req Slayer 90, Combat 90, started Mourning's End Part II; required item: none)
Targets: Dark beast (combat 182) — only variant, no higher-level alternative listed
Unattended difficulty: easy — dark beasts are PERMANENTLY aggressive (never lose interest, unlike most monsters), attack with melee unless they can't reach the player (then Magic), single-combat at both locations, so no baiting/re-aggro logic needed at all
1. Mourner Tunnels — Dark beast lvl 182; coords ~2028,4660,0 (cluster 1988-2033,4632-4664, mapID 10113); spawns 19; single (not multicombat); safespot no; access: item teleport "Slayer ring: Dark Beasts" (or the eternal/unlimited-charge Slayer ring) → lands 2028,4636, directly adjacent to the spawn cluster, effectively a one-click trip; needs: Mourning's End Part II started (same as the task's own otherreq, so already satisfied); hazards: not safespottable, so melee exposure is direct — Combat 90 + decent Prayer/HP sustain expected given the task's own gate
2. Iorwerth Dungeon — Dark beast lvl 182; coords ~3158,12427,0 (cluster 3158-3234,12385-12431, mapID 34); spawns 10; single; safespot no; access: no direct item teleport found in vendor TSV to Iorwerth Dungeon itself — would need a walk/fairy-ring route into Prifddinas then down; needs: none beyond task req; hazards: same melee exposure as above; bonus: kills here have a chance to drop crystal shards (irrelevant to routine choice, just a wiki note)
Recommend: 1 — Mourner Tunnels: a Slayer ring teleport (item, no spellbook dependency) lands within a couple of tiles of the spawn cluster, almost double the spawn count of Iorwerth, and the monster's permanent aggression means the routine never has to manage re-engagement

## Drakes (amount 50-110; req Slayer 84, Combat 90; required item: Boots of stone / Boots of brimstone / Granite boots, unless Elite Kourend & Kebos Diary is complete)
Targets: Drake (combat 192, 256.2 xp); Guardian Drake (superior variant, only if superiors enabled — has an extra stun special attack, flinch/ranged recommended)
Unattended difficulty: medium — single-way-combat dungeon (no multi-aggro) with a known damage/heat rotation, but the volcanic breath attack and mandatory heat-protection boots are both easy to get wrong unattended
1. Karuulm Slayer Dungeon, middle level, task-only Drake room (recommended by the wiki for being the smaller of the two rooms) — Drake lvl 192; coords 1195,10189,0 (from SlayerLocations.java; this page carries no separate LocLine coordinate table, only a room description); spawns 7 (task-only, vs. 7 in the normal/shared room); single (whole dungeon is single-way combat); safespot ?: not stated by source; access: item teleport "Rada's blessing 3/4: Mount Karuulm" → lands 1311,3806 right outside the dungeon entrance, walk in and down to the middle level; fallback: fairy ring code CIR then walk north up the mountain; needs: heat-protection boots (Boots of stone/brimstone/Granite boots) worn past the entrance chamber, OR Elite Kourend & Kebos Diary complete to skip that; Rada's blessing 3+ itself requires the Kourend & Kebos HARD diary to unlock; hazards: extreme heat (4 dmg/tick) without the boots, volcanic breath every ~7 auto-attacks (6-8 dmg x4 hits, avoidable by stepping off the targeted tile or dragonfire protection), ranged+ranged-melee attacks from the drake itself (Protect from Missiles blocks the normal attack)
2. Karuulm Slayer Dungeon, middle level, normal (shared, non-task-restricted) Drake room — same monster/level; coords unknown (same dungeon, separate room, no coordinates given by source); spawns 7 (normal); single; safespot ?: not stated; access/needs/hazards: identical to candidate 1, just a larger, more crowded room shared with off-task players
Recommend: 1 — same dungeon and access as the fallback, but the task-only room is explicitly called out as the better pick (smaller, and effectively private to players on this task); the real requirement to plan for is the heat-protection boots, not travel

## Dust Devils (amount 130-200, 200-250 with a 100-pt "Bigger and Badder"-style unlock; req Slayer 65, Combat 70, partial Desert Treasure I; required item: Facemask or Slayer helmet — needed only in the Smoke Dungeon, for smoke damage)
Targets: Dust devil (combat 93, 105 xp) — only variant that counts; Choke devil (combat 264, superior, spawns on death after the "Bigger and Badder" 50-pt unlock, not separately targetable)
Unattended difficulty: easy — non-wilderness options are both multicombat, cannonable-or-not aside, and safespottable; attacks are "ranged melee" (roll on Ranged defence) so Ranged/Magic gear stays effective
1. Smoke Dungeon, western task-only area (guarded by Dusty Aliv, off-task players turned away) — Dust devil combat 93; coords ~3179,9362,0 (cluster 3179-3261,9347-9402, no single mapID given); spawns 44 (by far the most of the three locations); multi; safespot yes; access: item teleport "Pollnivneach tablet" → lands 3340,3004, short walk SW to the smokey well entrance at 3310,2962, climb down; needs: Facemask or Slayer helmet (smoke damage protection — 20 dmg/20 ticks without it, though it cannot drop HP below 1); hazards: shares the dungeon with fire giants/pyrefiends/fire elementals outside the task-only pocket (irrelevant once inside it), Dusty Aliv blocks a cannon here specifically (moot, no cannon used)
2. Catacombs of Kourend — Dust devil combat 93; coords ~1708,10014,0 (cluster 1708-1718,10011-10035, same dungeon as the Dagannoth candidate, different section); spawns 13; multi; cannonable no (cannon banned dungeon-wide); safespot yes; access: same item teleport as the Dagannoth pick, "Xeric's talisman: Xeric's Heart" → 1644,3673 outside the King Rada I statue entrance, walk to the section with dust devils; needs: none — no facemask/Slayer helmet required here (no smoke mechanic in this dungeon); hazards: not task-restricted, so may be shared/crowded with off-task players farming other Catacombs monsters
3. Wilderness Slayer Cave (AVOID per routine policy) — Dust devil combat 93; coords ~3431,10117,0 (cluster 3431-3448,10117-10127); spawns 9 (fewest of the three); multi, cannonable yes, safespot yes; access: via the Wilderness Slayer Cave entrance (not detailed here); needs: none extra; hazards: level 25-28 Wilderness — PvP death risk to an unattended account, "do not bring items you are unwilling to lose" per the wiki's own wording; excluded from consideration per the AVOID-wilderness constraint
Recommend: 1 — Smoke Dungeon's task-only pocket: most spawns by far (44), safespottable, multicombat, no off-task crowding since Dusty Aliv excludes anyone not on this task, and a single clean item-teleport-plus-short-walk; only cost is wearing a facemask/Slayer helmet, which a Slayer routine likely already wears

## Elves (amount 110-170; req Combat 70, completion of Regicide; required item: none)
Targets: Iorwerth Archer (combat 90, ranged, Iorwerth Camp only); Iorwerth Warrior (combat 108, halberd/2-tile melee, Iorwerth Camp + Iorwerth Dungeon); Elf Archer/Elf Warrior (Lletya, need Mourning's End Part I progress, not just Regicide); Mourner lvl 108 only (Mourner HQ downstairs, needs Mourning's End Part I, full Mourner gear or gets attacked); Guard (Prifddinas, needs Song of the Elves) — none need a higher Slayer level (task has no Slayer level req at all)
Unattended difficulty: easy at Iorwerth Camp/Dungeon — single-combat, well-documented safespots, but archers/warriors are only aggressive during specific Song of the Elves story beats (passive otherwise, so the routine must initiate combat itself, not rely on aggro)
1. Iorwerth Dungeon — Iorwerth Warrior only, combat 108; coords ~3177,12401,0 (cluster 3177-3194,12399-12411, mapID 34); spawns 13, single type (uniform, higher xp than camp archers); single-combat; safespot yes (stand in the fabric-panel gaps on the room's west wall, warriors trap themselves 3 tiles north/south); access: item teleport "Teleport crystal: Prifddinas" (or Eternal version) → lands 3264,6066, walk to the Iorwerth Dungeon entrance within Prifddinas; needs: Song of the Elves completed (extra quest beyond the task's own Regicide requirement) — NOT guaranteed just from taking this task; hazards: none called out beyond the 2-tile halberd range; bonus crystal shard / enhanced crystal teleport seed drops here (not in the camp)
2. Iorwerth Camp — Iorwerth Archer (combat 90) + Iorwerth Warrior (combat 108), both count; coords ~2193,3244,0 (cluster spans roughly 2188-2210,3239-3261, mapID 0); spawns 6 archers + 6 warriors = 12; single-combat; safespot yes for warriors (use the camp's stools, keep 2+ tiles of separation since halberds reach 2 tiles) — archers not called out as safespottable (ranged, so less need); access: item teleport "Iorwerth camp teleport" scroll → lands 2194,3258, directly in camp; needs: only Regicide (the task's own requirement — no extra quest, so this is the reliably-available option); hazards: tents/barrels/crates clutter the camp and are called out as bad for cannon pathing (moot, no cannon used); archers/warriors passive outside certain Song of the Elves story beats, so the routine must engage them actively
3. Lletya — Elf Archer (1) + Elf Warrior (2), small count; coords 2329,3170 / 2329,3173 / 2345,3175,0; spawns 3 total (plus 1 more archer upstairs, not safespottable); single-combat; safespot yes downstairs (bench near entrance for warriors, wall near Arianwyn for the archer); access: item teleport "Teleport crystal: Lletya" (or Eternal) → lands 2330,3171, right in the village; needs: enough Mourning's End Part I progress to access Lletya (a quest gate beyond plain Regicide); hazards: very low spawn count (3-4) limits throughput regardless of safety
4. Prifddinas gates/market/Tower of Voices — Guard, combat 108, scattered 2-8 per spot across 8 separate pin clusters (South/East/North/West gate, market, Tower of Voices floors 0 and 1); coords vary (e.g. South Gate 3261,6026 / East Gate 3318,6082 / North Gate 3261,6133, mapID 29); spawns 2-8 per individual spot (fragmented, not a single dense room); single-combat; safespot yes at most spots (luring against gates/trees/stalls), NO at the Tower of Voices upper floor; access: same "Teleport crystal: Prifddinas" as candidate 1, walking to whichever gate; needs: Song of the Elves; hazards: none major, but low per-site density means more walking between kills than the dungeon or camp
Recommend: 2 — Iorwerth Camp: a direct item teleport lands inside the camp, decent combined spawn count (12), documented warrior safespots, and — unlike every other candidate here — needs nothing beyond the task's own Regicide requirement, so it works even if Song of the Elves/Mourning's End haven't been done; swap to candidate 1 (Iorwerth Dungeon) if Song of the Elves is already complete, since it's a tighter, more uniform, cannonable room with better drops

## Fire Giants (amount 130-200; req Combat 65; required item: none generally — some sites add their own, see below)
Targets: Fire giant, two level variants (combat 104 and 109 at Catacombs/Giants' Den — 109s preferred for xp, more HP/less Defence; combat 86 everywhere else); Branda the Fire Queen (Royal Titans boss, off-task alternative, not covered here)
Unattended difficulty: easy — this monster has more viable non-wilderness candidate sites than almost any other task, several task-restricted and safespottable
1. Stronghold Slayer Cave — Fire giant combat 86; coords ~2391,9781,0 (cluster 2391-2415,9769-9785, mapID 19); spawns 12, in groups of 3 each separated by its own safespot; single-combat (not multi); safespot yes (per-group); access: item teleport "Slayer ring: Stronghold" → lands 2431,3422 right outside the cave, walk in; needs: a Slayer task is required to even enter (i.e., task-restricted — no off-task crowding); hazards: none called out
2. Catacombs of Kourend — Fire giant combat 104/109 (higher-xp variants, drop Ancient shards/Dark totem pieces); coords ~1622,10057,0 (cluster 1618-1637,10048-10067, mapID 32); spawns 12; multi; cannonable no (banned dungeon-wide); safespot yes (two documented spots, one for aggressive/one for tolerant giants); access: same "Xeric's talisman: Xeric's Heart" teleport used for Dagannoth/Dust devils → 1644,3673, walk to this section; needs: none extra; hazards: multicombat exposure away from the safespot, shared dungeon (not task-restricted)
3. Giants' Den — Fire giant combat 104/109 (same higher-xp variants and drop table as Catacombs); coords ~1451,9900,0 (cluster 1451-1461,9894-9908, mapID unlisted "-1"); spawns 8; single-combat (not multi, unlike Catacombs); cannonable yes (moot, no cannon); safespot yes (two spots documented, entrance and mid-room by the fire pillar); access: no direct item teleport found in vendor TSV — reachable via a Catacombs of Kourend vine-climb entrance per the dungeon's own transportation section, or a surface route into Giants' Den; needs: none extra; hazards: none called out
4. Karuulm Slayer Dungeon, upper level — Fire giant combat 86; coords ~1125,10275,0 (cluster 1125-1143,10258-10279, mapID 33); spawns 13 (highest count of the non-wilderness options); single-combat; safespot yes (stand in the entrance hallway); access: same "Rada's blessing 3/4: Mount Karuulm" teleport used for Drakes → 1311,3806; needs: Boots of stone/brimstone/Granite boots unless Elite Kourend & Kebos Diary complete (same heat mechanic as the Drakes candidate); hazards: extreme heat without the boots, same as Drakes
Recommend: 1 — Stronghold Slayer Cave: task-restricted entry (nobody off-task competing for spawns), single-combat groups each with their own safespot, a one-click Slayer-ring teleport landing right outside, and no extra required items or quest gates at all

## Fossil Island Wyverns (amount 20-50, 55-75 with "Wyver-nother two" 100-pt unlock; req Slayer 66, Combat 60, completion of Bone Voyage AND Elemental Workshop I; required item: Elemental shield, Mind shield, Dragonfire shield, Dragonfire ward, or Ancient wyvern shield — anti-dragon shield and antifire potions do NOT work against the icy breath)
Targets: Spitting Wyvern (combat 139, weak ranged instead of full breath — best for a quick/simple fight); Long-tailed Wyvern (combat 147, typeless ranged only when out of melee range — fight in melee); Taloned Wyvern (combat 152, typeless ranged even in melee — the wiki explicitly says avoid); Ancient Wyvern (combat 210, needs Slayer 82, typeless ranged from any range, stronger — only relevant at higher Slayer)
Unattended difficulty: medium — neither site is safespottable or multicombat, so this is direct melee/ranged/magic tanking the whole task; the required anti-icy-breath shield is a hard gate (a wrong/missing shield choice — e.g. anti-dragon shield — silently does nothing)
1. Task-only Wyvern Cave, inside the Mushroom Forest — all four wyvern types; coords ~3678,3854,0 (northern cave pin); spawns 4 Spitting + 4 Taloned + 4 Long-tailed + 3 Ancient (one more Ancient than the southern cave); single-combat; cannonable no; safespot no; access: item teleport "Digsite pendant: Fossil Island" → lands 3763,3869 (plane 1, Museum Camp; requires Bone Voyage, already a task req), walk through the Mushroom Forest to the northern cave; needs: a Fossil Island Wyverns Slayer task (task-restricted entry, so no off-task competition); hazards: icy breath (freeze risk without an Ancient wyvern shield specifically — the other three shields reduce damage but don't prevent freezing), melee+ranged combined attacks, no safespot to fall back on
2. Wyvern Cave, east of Museum Camp (southern, open to everyone) — same four wyvern types; coords ~3746,3779,0 (southern cave pin); spawns 4 Spitting + 4 Taloned + 4 Long-tailed + 2 Ancient; single-combat; cannonable no; safespot no; access: same Digsite pendant teleport → 3763,3869, shorter walk than the task-only cave (no Mushroom Forest crossing); needs: none beyond the task's own requirements — open to all players, not task-restricted; hazards: identical combat hazards to candidate 1, plus potential crowding/competition for spawns since anyone can be here
Recommend: 1 — the task-only cave: task-restricted so no off-task crowding for a routine running unattended for hours, one extra Ancient Wyvern spawn, and the only real cost is a longer walk through the Mushroom Forest from the same Digsite pendant landing
