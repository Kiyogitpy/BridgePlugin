# Kuradal/Duradel slayer task site candidates

Account constraints: Lunar spellbook (no standard-spellbook teleports; tablets/jewellery OK), no dwarf cannon, melee/ranged unattended, 130-200+ kills/task, avoid Wilderness unless no alternative.

## Aberrant Spectres (amount 130-200; req 60 Slayer/65 combat/Priest in Peril; required item: Nose peg or Slayer helmet)
Targets: Aberrant spectre (96); Abhorrent spectre (253, superior, needs Bigger and Badder unlock); Deviant spectre (169, Catacombs of Kourend variant, needs no extra Slayer lvl); Repugnant spectre (335, superior of Deviant)
Unattended difficulty: easy — safespottable, no dragonfire/poison, only a stat-drain special countered by nose peg/Slayer helmet
1. Slayer Tower, 1st floor — Aberrant spectre lvl 96; coords 3413,3550,plane1; spawns 14; single-combat; safespot yes (walls/brick piles/armour stands, keep 6 tiles); access: Slayer ring "Slayer Tower" teleport (3421,3537,0, needs Priest in Peril) then up stairs to 1st floor, or Priest in Peril unlocks the shortcut entrance; needs: Priest in Peril (already required for the task), nose peg/Slayer helmet; hazards: none major, stat-drain if unprotected
2. Stronghold Slayer Cave — Aberrant spectre lvl 96; coords 2443,9785,0 (approx, cluster ~2440-2471,9774-9794); spawns 13; single-combat; safespot only after aggression timer runs out; access: Slayer ring "Stronghold" teleport (2431,3422,0, no quest needed) then in to cave, Nieve/Steve slayer master right outside, bank close by; needs: nose peg/Slayer helmet; hazards: none major; cannonable but no cannon on this account (moot)
3. Catacombs of Kourend — Deviant spectre lvl 169; coords ~1646,9988,0 (cluster 1602-1657,9987-10015); spawns 12; multicombat; safespot yes (documented safe spot NW of spawns, picture guide exists); access: task-only area, entrance via trapdoor near Kourend catacombs guardian (no direct item teleport found in vendor TSV; nearest is Rada's blessing: Kourend Woodland 1554,3461,0 then walk); needs: nose peg/Slayer helmet, catacombs entry fee (coins, standard slayer task access); hazards: multicombat area, lower kill/hr due to higher defence
4. Deepfin Mine — Aberrant spectre lvl 96; coords 2051,9208,0 (cluster); spawns 7; single-combat; safespot yes (eastern side near standing torch); access: no item teleport identified in vendor TSV (reached via Agility shortcut, 84 Agility, or walk-in); needs: nose peg/Slayer helmet, high Agility for the bank-chest shortcut; hazards: small spawn count, low priority
Recommend: 2 — Stronghold Slayer Cave: easiest reliable teleport (Slayer ring, no quest gate beyond task's own Priest in Peril), Slayer master and bank adjacent, single-combat, safespottable once aggro wears off, good spawn density (13).

## Abyssal Demons (amount 130-200; req 85 Slayer/85 combat/Priest in Peril or Fairytale II; required item: none listed, but Teleport anchoring scroll strongly advised)
Targets: Abyssal demon (124); Greater abyssal demon (342, superior, needs Bigger and Badder unlock); Abyssal Sire (350, optional boss alternative, not a normal kill loop)
Unattended difficulty: hard — abyssal demons randomly self-teleport (and can teleport the PLAYER up to 20 tiles away) unless a Teleport anchoring scroll is permanently unlocked; none of the 4 sites is both single-combat AND safespottable AND non-hazardous
1. Catacombs of Kourend (basement-style area) — Abyssal demon lvl 124; coords ~1669,10054,0 (cluster 1669-1678,10054-10098); spawns 13; multi-combat; safespot no; access: task-only area, entrance via Kourend trapdoor, no direct item teleport in vendor TSV (nearest: Rada's blessing Kourend Woodland 1554,3461,0, then walk/descend); needs: Priest in Peril or Fairytale II (task req); hazards: multicombat, demon teleport mechanic, no safespot — the wiki-recommended location for barraging but relies on AoE magic, not idle melee/ranged
2. Slayer Tower, basement — Abyssal demon lvl 124; coords ~3427,9965,0 (cluster 3427-3445,9954-9974); spawns 14; single-combat; safespot yes but ONLY with Teleport anchoring scroll permanent unlock; access: Slayer ring "Slayer Tower" teleport (3421,3537,0, needs Priest in Peril) then down to basement; needs: task-only entry, Priest in Peril; hazards: without the anchoring scroll unlock, demon's random teleport can strand the player far from the safespot — unattended risk
3. Wilderness Slayer Cave — Abyssal demon lvl 124; coords ~3335,10161,0 (cluster 3335-3347,10158-10168); spawns 8; multi-combat; safespot no; access: Wilderness level 29-32, task-only chamber; needs: none extra; hazards: WILDERNESS — PvP risk, avoid per instructions
4. Abyssal Area (Abyss) — Abyssal demon lvl 124; coords ~3016-3061,4874-4917,0; spawns 13; not multi; safespot no; access: via Abyss entrance (Mage of Zamorak / abyss minigame mechanic), no item teleport in vendor TSV; needs: none extra; hazards: other aggressive abyssal creatures present, wiki explicitly calls this "not efficient" outside Konar tasks
Recommend: SKIP or 2 with caution — every site has a real unattended hazard (random self/player teleport, multicombat AoE-oriented Catacombs, or Wilderness). If forced to pick, Slayer Tower basement (2) is closest to safe IF a Teleport anchoring scroll has been permanently unlocked first; otherwise this task is not well suited to unattended melee/ranged and should be flagged to the user before automating.

## Ankou (amount 50-80; req 40 combat, none else; required item: none)
Targets: Ankou (lvl 75/82/86/95/98, five near-identical variants, all count); Dark Ankou (95, Skotizo boss-fight only — not a normal farming target)
Unattended difficulty: easy — melee only, low max hit (8-10), Protect from Melee negates it fully, Stronghold of Security site is entirely non-wilderness and non-multicombat
1. Stronghold of Security: Sepulchre of Death — Ankou lvl 75-98 mixed; coords ~1963-2011,4907-4957,0; spawns 32 (largest of all sites); single-combat; safespot yes (south side of the SE-most skeleton, south-west room); access: no direct item teleport found in vendor TSV for Stronghold of Security (F2P minigame area entered near Falador/Varrock-ish entrance — walk in, no quest); needs: none; hazards: none — F2P safe minigame area, not wilderness
2. Catacombs of Kourend — Ankou mixed variants; coords ~1637,9991,0 (cluster 1637-1648,9990-9999); spawns 10; multi-combat; safespot no; access: task-only area, no direct item teleport in vendor TSV (nearest Rada's blessing: Kourend Woodland 1554,3461,0, then walk to entrance); needs: none; hazards: multicombat, no safespot, but bonecrusher heals prayer here
3. The Forgotten Cemetery — Ankou mixed; coords ~2960-2992,3739-3766,0; spawns 21; single-combat; safespot yes (behind SW coffin, between bone spawns); access: SlayerLocations.java pins this at wilderness level "30" (WILDERNESS site, wiki's own Notes column doesn't call this out explicitly but the location is inside the Wilderness) — nearest teleport: Amulet of glory Edgeville (3087,3496,0) then walk north; needs: none; hazards: WILDERNESS — PvP risk, avoid per instructions despite good spawn/safespot stats
4. Stronghold Slayer Cave — Ankou mixed; coords ~2468-2483,9797-9808,0; spawns 8; single-combat; safespot no; access: Slayer ring "Stronghold" teleport (2431,3422,0); needs: none; hazards: crowded (popular cannon spot), smallest spawn count
Recommend: 1 — Stronghold of Security: Sepulchre of Death: largest spawn pool (32), single-combat, safespottable, zero wilderness ambiguity, F2P-safe area, only drawback is no vendor-TSV item teleport listed (walk-in).

## Aquanites (amount 30-50; req 78 Slayer/73 Sailing/80 Slayer reward points "Lured In" unlock; required item: none, but a slash weapon recommended to sever the lure)
Targets: Aquanite (lvl unlisted numerically, magic attacker); Elder Aquanite (superior, needs Bigger and Badder, 1/200 chance — has a 35-dmg special that punishes long-held Protect from Magic, needs prayer-flicking)
Unattended difficulty: medium — only ONE location exists (no comparison table on this page, single site), single-way combat, safespottable, but the Elder Aquanite superior variant's special attack specifically punishes a static "always-on Protect from Magic" bot pattern (prayer-flicking needed), and access itself is a boat trip
1. Ynysdail Cavern (only known site) — Aquanite; coords 2248,9862,0 (SlayerLocations.java); spawns ?; single-combat ("single-way combat area"); safespot yes (9-10 tile attack range, screenshot exists on wiki: "Aquanite safespot.png"); access: NOT a standard item teleport — requires sailing a boat with adamant keel+ to Ynysdail (first visit) or, after building the rowboat hotspot (70 Construction) and completing Song of the Elves, a rowboat north of Gwenith; no entry in vendor teleportation_items.tsv for this location; needs: 78 Slayer, 73 Sailing, 80 Slayer reward points spent on "Lured In" unlock, ideally Song of the Elves + rowboat built for repeat trips; hazards: no Dwarf multicannon possible (ground too damp — moot, no cannon anyway), Elder Aquanite's prayer-punish special is a real unattended risk without prayer-flick logic
Recommend: 1 — SKIP unless Sailing/rowboat travel is already implemented; it is the only site so there is no real "choice," but the Sailing-boat access path (not a bank teleport, not in the vendor TSV) and the Elder Aquanite's prayer-flick requirement make this one of the least automation-friendly tasks on this list without dedicated travel + prayer-flick logic.

## Araxytes (amount 60-80; req 92 Slayer/Priest in Peril; required item: none, but venom immunity strongly recommended)
Targets: Araxyte lvl 96 (worse drops, cannot spawn superior — AVOID on Duradel/Kuradal tasks per wiki advice); Araxyte lvl 146 (preferred, spawns Dreadborn Araxyte superior); Dreadborn Araxyte (281, superior, venom-pool special up to 50 dmg); Araxxor (890, optional boss alternative)
Unattended difficulty: medium — only one location, multicombat + cannonable + safespottable, but attacks apply venom (needs venom immunity/extended anti-venom+ upkeep) and the level 96 sub-variant should be avoided in favor of 146s (routine needs to be selective, not just "attack nearest")
1. Morytania Spider Cave (only known site) — Araxyte lvl 96 (main chamber, avoid) / lvl 146 (northern single-way on-task cave, preferred) / lvl 96+146 mixed (eastern/southern multi-way on-task caves); coords 3657,3407,0 (cave entrance); spawns "a lot" (uncounted); multicombat (yes, though a single-way sub-cave with only 146s exists); safespot yes; access: the wiki's own recommended "spider cave teleport" is an ARCEUUS spellbook spell, NOT usable on this account's Lunar spellbook — nearest item teleport is Ectophial to Ectofuntus (3660,3522,0) then walk south (~115 tiles), or Morytania legs to Burgh de Rott (3482,3230,0) then walk further; no direct entry in vendor teleportation_items.tsv; needs: Priest in Peril, 92 Slayer, venom immunity (extended anti-venom+ recommended for a long unattended trip); hazards: venom (6+ dmg/tick starting), Dreadborn Araxyte's venom-pool special (up to 50 dmg) if a superior spawns while unattended — needs damage-mitigation logic, not wilderness though
Recommend: 1 — it is the only site; use the single-way northern sub-cave (146-only) if reachable for safety/consistency, otherwise the main/multi-way caves are still non-wilderness and safespottable. Ensure venom immunity is always active before starting.

## Aviansie (amount 120-200; req Death Plateau quest + "Watch the birdie" 80 Slayer reward points, 60 Agility or 60 Strength; required item: Ranged or Magic weapon (flying, melee cannot hit), Armadylean god-aligned item recommended for tolerance)
Targets: Aviansie lvl 69-148 (God Wars Dungeon antechamber); Aviansie lvl 69-137 (Wilderness GWD, avoid); Kree'arra (580)/Flight Kilisa/Wingman Skree/Flockleader Geerin (God Wars boss room, needs 70 Ranged+crossbow+mith grapple to access) — Armadylean spiritual warrior/ranger/mage do NOT count
Unattended difficulty: hard — the only non-wilderness site is multicombat AND not safespottable, meaning several flying aviansie can gang up on the player at once; ranged/magic only (no melee)
1. God Wars Dungeon (antechamber, normal) — Aviansie lvl 69-148; coords ~2833-2878,5259-5310,0 (cluster; SlayerLocations.java entrance at 3016,3739,0 surface); spawns 36; multi-combat; safespot no; access: no direct GWD item teleport in vendor TSV — nearest is walking from the Trollheim tablet (2890,3679,0) area or from the surface entrance near Forgotten Cemetery (non-wilderness side), then down into the dungeon; needs: Death Plateau, "Watch the birdie" unlock, Armadylean item for tolerance, Ranged or Magic gear; hazards: multicombat with NO safespot — several fliers can attack simultaneously, real risk for an unattended bot without a strong AoE/tank setup
2. Wilderness God Wars Dungeon — Aviansie lvl 69-137; coords ~2938-2951,10106-10123,0; spawns 8; multi-combat; safespot no; access: Wilderness level ~28 entrance; needs: same as above; hazards: WILDERNESS — PvP risk, avoid per instructions, on top of the same no-safespot multicombat issue
3. Kree'arra boss room (God Wars Dungeon) — Kree'arra/Flight Kilisa/Wingman Skree/Flockleader Geerin; coords same general GWD dungeon area, boss room gated; spawns fixed boss instance; not a normal trash-mob farm; access: needs 70 Ranged, crossbow + mith grapple to cross the chasm, Zarosian item recommended to bank safely at Nex's area; needs: significant additional requirements; hazards: boss mechanics unsuited to a simple unattended kill loop
Recommend: SKIP or 1 with heavy caution — every real (non-boss) site is multicombat with no safespot, meaning an idle ranged/magic bot can be swarmed by multiple flying aviansie; this task has no genuinely safe unattended farming spot in the data available. If automated anyway, God Wars Dungeon antechamber (1) is the only non-wilderness option.

## Basilisks (amount 130-200; req 40 Slayer/20 Defence/40 combat + "Basilocked" 80 Slayer reward points; required item: Mirror shield or V's shield (mandatory — unprotected gaze deals immense damage and drains stats))
Targets: Basilisk lvl 61 (max hit 5, inaccurate melee); Monstrous basilisk (135, superior, needs Bigger and Badder); Basilisk Knight (204, needs The Fremennik Exiles, melee+magic up to 20 each, matches player attack speed); Basilisk Sentinel (358, superior of knight, slow projectile encase-in-stone special)
Unattended difficulty: easy (plain basilisks) / medium (knights) — mirror shield negates the gaze entirely, both sites are single-combat and safespottable, no wilderness; cannon cannot be placed anywhere here (moot, no cannon)
1. Fremennik Slayer Dungeon — Basilisk lvl 61; coords 2738-2746,10002-10018,0 (SlayerLocations.java: 2738,10008,0); spawns 8; single-combat; safespot yes; access: Slayer ring "Fremennik" teleport (2802,9999,0, no quest) then walk in; needs: Mirror shield/V's shield; hazards: none major, low max hit (5) if shielded — slow/low-value task (thin drop table) but very safe
2. Jormungand's Prison — Basilisk lvl 61 (12 spawns) and Basilisk Knight lvl 204 (20 spawns) together; coords ~2403-2485,10375-10410,0; spawns 12 basilisks + 20 knights; single-combat; safespot yes for melee attacks (knights still retaliate with magic — protection prayer can't block both attack types simultaneously); access: only reachable after The Fremennik Exiles (no direct item teleport listed in vendor TSV; nearest is the Fremennik Slayer Dungeon entrance then further in, or via quest-specific access); needs: Mirror shield/V's shield, The Fremennik Exiles completed; hazards: knights hit up to 20 melee/magic and match attack speed — meaningfully harder than plain basilisks, max ~100 kills/hr due to high defence
Recommend: 1 — Fremennik Slayer Dungeon: simplest, closest reliable Slayer-ring teleport, single-combat, safespottable, and does not require The Fremennik Exiles quest. Site 2 (Jormungand's Prison) is a fine upgrade if that quest is already done, for the jaw drop and higher combat level variety, but is a harder unattended fight.

## Black Demons (amount 130-200; req 80 combat; required item: none, but Protect from Melee/safespot needed vs. their high stats)
Targets: Black demon lvl 172-188 (standard, all listed non-boss locations count); Demonic gorilla (275, alternate for non-Krystilia/Konar tasks, needs Monkey Madness II, far harder); Balfrug Kreeyath/Skotizo/Porazdir/Kolodion (situational boss-ish alternates, not farming spots)
Unattended difficulty: easy (Taverley) — safespottable, cannonable (no cannon on this account so moot), demons are not fast/dangerous once safespotted with Protect from Melee or ranged/magic from range
1. Taverley Dungeon — Black demon lvl 172; coords ~2826-2875,9763-9801,0 (cluster); spawns 24 (most of any non-wilderness site); single-combat; safespot yes (SW stalagmites or NE crevice, picture guide exists, good for cannon multicombat too — though needs Dusty Key or 70 Agility for the NE shortcut); access: direct Taverley tablet teleport (2893,3465,0) then a short walk down into the dungeon; needs: none extra (Dusty Key/70 Agility only for the alternate NE safespot, not required to fight here); hazards: none major
2. Chasm of Fire (Bottom Level) — Black demon lvl 172; coords ~1356-1389,9869-9901,0; spawns 17; multi-combat; safespot yes (stalagmites north, or centre near Voice of Yama); access: no direct item teleport in vendor TSV found; needs: reachable from Kourend area (walk); hazards: multicombat but safespottable; A Kingdom Divided + Yama contract signed adds bonus value but isn't required
3. Catacombs of Kourend — Black demon lvl 178-184; coords ~1718-1721,10079-10090,0; spawns 4 (only reaches 4 of the spawns per wiki note "not possible to utilise all four spawns" efficiently); multi-combat; safespot yes; access: task-only area, no direct item teleport (Rada's blessing Kourend Woodland 1554,3461,0 then walk); needs: none; hazards: higher defence variant here, wiki says "generally not worth killing them there"
4. Brimhaven Dungeon — Black demon lvl 172; coords 2700-2715,9479-9489,0; spawns 4; single-combat; safespot yes (near agility pipe, cannon 2-3 of 4); access: Brimhaven tablet (2757,3179,0) then walk in; needs: none; hazards: small spawn count
Recommend: 1 — Taverley Dungeon: best spawn density (24) among safe non-wilderness sites, single-combat, safespottable, and reachable by a direct Taverley teleport tablet already in the vendor list — the strongest access+safety combination on this list.

## Black Dragons (amount 10-20; req 80 combat, partial Dragon Slayer I; required item: Anti-dragon shield or Dragonfire shield or Antifire potion(4) — MANDATORY dragonfire protection)
Targets: Baby black dragon (83, no dragonfire, weakest); Black dragon lvl 227 (standard, dragonfire); Black dragon lvl 247 (Wilderness Slayer Cave only, avoid); King Black Dragon (276, boss, does NOT count for Krystilia); Brutal black dragon (318, needs 77 Slayer, extra magic attack, harder)
Unattended difficulty: hard — every real target breathes dragonfire (short-ranged for standard, long-ranged for brutals) and low task amount (10-20) means low total risk-exposure but antifire-potion UPTIME is a genuine unattended concern (potions expire; a bot needs to reapply, which this repo's constraints flag as unsuitable-unattended by default)
1. Taverley Dungeon, upper level (task-only area) — Baby black dragon (5) + Black dragon lvl 227 (12); coords ~2774-2807,9611-9649,0; spawns 17 total (12 adult + 5 baby, best density here); single-combat; safespot yes; access: direct Taverley tablet teleport (2893,3465,0) then walk down/in to the task-only upper area; needs: anti-dragon shield/DFS + antifire potion(4) (extended antifire recommended for longer uptime), partial Dragon Slayer I (task req); hazards: dragonfire — antifire uptime must be actively maintained; baby dragons alone (no dragonfire) are a lower-risk subset if the routine can selectively target them
2. Taverley Dungeon, lower level — Black dragon lvl 227; coords 2829,9826 / 2835,9824,0; spawns 2; single-combat; safespot yes (ladder as blockade); access: same Taverley tablet teleport then walk further; needs: same as above; hazards: same dragonfire concern, very small spawn count (2)
3. Corsair Cove Dungeon — Black dragon lvl 227 (2) + Baby black dragon (1); coords 1942,8967 / 1946,8971,0; spawns 3; single-combat; safespot yes; access: needs Dragon Slayer II completed, closest to a bank per wiki (Mythical cape teleport mentioned, not in vendor TSV); needs: Dragon Slayer II; hazards: dragonfire, small spawn count, extra quest requirement
4. Evil Chicken's Lair — Black dragon lvl 227 (4) + Baby black dragon (1); coords 2448-2460,4359-4376,0; spawns 5; single-combat; safespot yes (baby dragon too); access: needs partial Recipe for Disaster (Freeing Sir Amik Varze) + raw chicken sacrifice + rope for baby dragon access, near Zanaris bank; needs: quest partial completion, consumable items each visit; hazards: dragonfire, extra per-visit item consumption (raw chicken)
Recommend: 1 — Taverley Dungeon upper level: best spawn count (17) of any non-wilderness site, single-combat, safespottable, reachable via the existing Taverley tablet teleport, and task-only. HOWEVER flag to the user: dragonfire + antifire-potion uptime is exactly the "unsuitable unattended" pattern called out in the task constraints — a routine here needs reliable potion-reapplication logic or it will eventually take unprotected dragonfire hits over a 130-200-kill-equivalent (here 10-20) unattended session. Baby-only targeting (no dragonfire) is the safer fallback within the same site.

## Bloodveld (amount 130-200; req 50 Slayer/50 combat/Priest in Peril; required item: none, but magic-defence armour recommended (their melee is magic-based))
Targets: Bloodveld lvl 76 (standard); Bloodveld lvl 81 (God Wars variant, Zamorak-aligned, aggressive w/o god item, no superior); Insatiable Bloodveld (202, superior, needs Bigger and Badder); Mutated Bloodveld (123, better drops, 4 locations); Insatiable mutated Bloodveld (278, superior of mutated); Reanimated bloodveld (Arceuus spell alternative, not usable — off Lunar spellbook)
Unattended difficulty: easy — magic-based melee is negated fully by Protect from Melee, most good sites are safespottable and non-wilderness, and the Stronghold Slayer Cave / Slayer Tower sites need no extra quests
1. Stronghold Slayer Cave — Bloodveld lvl 76; coords ~2434-2489,9817-9834,0; spawns 12; single-combat; safespot yes (8 mapped safespots, picture guide); access: Slayer ring "Stronghold" teleport (2431,3422,0, no quest) then in, close to bank and Nieve; needs: none; hazards: none major
2. Catacombs of Kourend — Mutated Bloodveld lvl 123 (3 clusters: NE=7, SE=5, SW=6); coords ~1625-1694,10013-10077,0; spawns 18 (largest pool); multi-combat; safespot yes (SW cluster safespot documented, goading potion suits NE/SE clusters); access: task-only area, no direct item teleport in vendor TSV (Rada's blessing Kourend Woodland 1554,3461,0 then walk); needs: none; hazards: SW cluster has wandering possessed pickaxes as ambient nuisance, multicombat
3. Slayer Tower, 1st floor — Bloodveld lvl 76; coords ~3409-3426,3557-3576,1; spawns 15; single-combat; safespot yes; access: Slayer ring "Slayer Tower" teleport (3421,3537,0, needs Priest in Peril, already a task req) then up; needs: none extra; hazards: none major, gets Morytania Diary bonus XP
4. Slayer Tower, basement — Bloodveld lvl 76; coords ~3403-3423,9929-9949,0; spawns 12; single-combat; safespot yes; access: same Slayer ring teleport then down; needs: task-only entry (less crowded); hazards: none major
Recommend: 1 — Stronghold Slayer Cave: simplest reliable teleport, single-combat, well-documented safespots, close to bank/Slayer master, no extra requirements beyond the task's own. Catacombs of Kourend (2) is the higher-throughput multicombat alternative for Mutated Bloodveld if better drops are wanted.

## Blue Dragons (amount 110-170; req 65 combat, partial Dragon Slayer I; required item: Anti-dragon shield or Dragonfire shield or Antifire potion(4) — MANDATORY dragonfire protection)
Targets: Baby blue dragon (48, no dragonfire, weakest); Blue dragon lvl 111 (standard, dragonfire+melee); Brutal blue dragon (271, needs Catacombs or Ruins of Tapoyauik, extra magic attack + long-ranged dragonfire, harder); Vorkath (boss, one-off during Dragon Slayer II then repeatable, not a farming site)
Unattended difficulty: hard — dragonfire on every non-baby target; same antifire-uptime concern as Black Dragons, though max hit is somewhat lower than black/brutal variants; the biggest/best site needs a Dusty Key or 70 Agility
1. Taverley Dungeon, lower level — Blue dragon lvl 111 (9 adults) + Baby blue dragon (12 babies); coords ~2897-2925,9763-9808,0 (SlayerLocations.java pins 2897,9797,0 exactly here); spawns 21 total (best density); single-combat; safespot yes; access: direct Taverley tablet teleport (2893,3465,0) then a short walk to the dungeon entrance; needs: Dusty key OR 70 Agility to reach this chamber, anti-dragon shield/DFS + antifire potion(4); hazards: dragonfire — antifire uptime must be actively maintained; baby-only targeting removes the dragonfire risk entirely at this same site
2. Taverley Dungeon, upper level (task-only) — Blue dragon lvl 111; coords ~2889-2955,9612-9635,0; spawns 12 adults; single-combat; safespot yes; access: same Taverley tablet then further in; needs: 70 Agility shortcut or Dusty key, same dragonfire protection; hazards: same as above, task-only so less crowded
3. Isle of Souls Dungeon — Blue dragon lvl 111 (4) + Baby blue dragon (3); coords ~2120-2131,9298-9306,0; spawns 7; single-combat; safespot yes; access: no direct item teleport in vendor TSV found (walk from Isle of Souls); needs: none extra; hazards: dragonfire, smaller spawn count
4. Ogre Enclave — Blue dragon lvl 111; coords ~2568-2609,9431-9461,0; spawns 6 adults; single-combat; safespot yes; access: needs Watchtower quest; Ring of dueling to Ferox Enclave (3151,3635,0) is NOT the right destination — wiki recommends Ring of dueling to Castle Wars then run east to Gu'Tanoth for banking, no direct dungeon-entrance teleport in vendor TSV; needs: Watchtower completed; hazards: dragonfire, dragons "very far apart" per wiki (slower kill rate)
Recommend: 1 — Taverley Dungeon lower level: best spawn density (21, mixed adults/babies), single-combat, safespottable, reachable via the existing Taverley tablet teleport. Same antifire-uptime caveat as Black Dragons applies to the adult dragons here — flag to the user; baby-only targeting at this same site is dragonfire-free and much safer for unattended running if acceptable for task credit.
